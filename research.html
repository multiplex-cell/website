Research

We work at the interface of genome engineering and cell therapy, developing next-generation tools for multiplexed, precise editing of the human genome. Our goal is to push the boundaries of what can be written into a living cell — and to translate that capability into therapeutic strategies for disease.

01

Multiplex genome editing

Making many precise edits simultaneously in a single cell remains a fundamental challenge. We develop recombinase-based and prime-editing platforms that enable large-scale, programmable rearrangements of the human genome — from targeted insertions to chromosome-scale restructuring.

02

Hematopoietic stem cell engineering

Hematopoietic stem cells offer a unique route to durable, systemic gene therapy. We engineer HSCs with multiplexed edits to reprogram their progeny for targeted applications — including brain repair — exploiting the natural migratory capacity of HSC-derived cells.

03

Precision base editing and sensing

We develop and evolve base editors with reduced bystander activity, and programmable nucleic acid sensing platforms that link detection of genomic or transcriptomic signals to controlled gene expression in human cells.
